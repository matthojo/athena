# Getting help

- **How do I…?** Start with the [README](README.md) and the
  [Guide](GUIDE.md). If they don't answer it, ask in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/matthojo/athena/discussions).
- **Something's broken:** open a
  [bug report](https://github.com/matthojo/athena/issues/new/choose). Settings
  → About lists the details to copy into it.
- **An idea:** suggest it in
  [Discussions](https://github.com/matthojo/athena/discussions) or open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/matthojo/athena/issues/new/choose).
- **A security problem:** report it privately, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
  describes, never in a public issue.

Your copy holds your own notes, people and accounts. Before you post
anything, take out real names, addresses, numbers and screenshots of your
data. Sample mode (README, "Try it locally") is a safe way to show a
problem.

Athena is maintained in spare time, so there's no guaranteed response time.
