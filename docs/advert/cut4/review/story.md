# Story review, cut 3 (creative director)

Method: frames every 0.5 s, every 0.1 s at key moments, and some at full size. Phone legibility judged from 480px-wide thumbnails.

## Ranked issues (worst first)

**1. The Ask payoff can't be read (22.0–28.2).** The answer is 65 words in about 28px type on a card that takes up a third of the frame. On a phone that is about 6px. The full answer is up for only 1.8 s (26.4–28.2), so nobody gets past "Elena wants to test". Fix: cut the sample answer to one line ("Elena wants to try a higher price with new customers. She'd like your view by Friday."). Push in until the answer fills about 60% of the frame height. Change "Team price" to "Pro price", because cut 4 introduces a feature called Team.

**2. The day contradicts itself.** Home (12–18) shows a 10:40 flight to Lisbon, with a Lisbon car hire and hotel below it. After that the person has an ordinary day at home: mentoring at 16:00, an "evening run" that opens a playlist called **Morning run** (39.0), and then an **Evening walk** habit (41–44). Fix the sample data: keep only the train in the travel card (it is a commute), rename the playlist "Evening run", and make the habit "Run". Then the rotation's "run" pays off.

**3. In the Music beat, Athena does nothing (34.8–40.8, 6 s).** For 2.7 s the camera scrolls quickly through stats, charts and recent days, none of which can be read. Then a tap opens a track list. That's browsing, not help. Fix: land on Playlists straight away, tap "Evening run" and press "Open in Spotify". That's 3.6 s, and it frees 2.4 s.

**4. Health/Sleep is passive and in the wrong order (44.4–48.0).** The beat is "7h 6m a night, about the same as the 4 weeks before", from Saturday night, on a Monday evening, after the evening habit. Nothing happens and nothing changes. Fix: cut it. "Sleep" already flashes in the rotation, and Home could carry "Slept 7h 9m" in the morning greeting. This funds the Team beat.

**5. The rotation stops calling back to the day too early (53.4–61.2).** Only about 7 phrases can be read (53.4–56.8). After 57.1 they flicker as texture, which is right. But the callbacks to the day run out after "run". "Habit" (60.87) and "sleep" (61.1) fall where each phrase is up for about 100 ms, and "birthday" and "booking code" use up readable slots. Fix: make the first six readable slots retell the day in order: platform, pricing call, *the film* (the cut 4 Saved), gift idea (Team), run, streak. Drop the later duplicate "film" (57.1).

**6. The opening is slow and abstract (0–9.6).** It takes 9.6 s to reach real UI. The eight unlabelled icons can't be decoded on first viewing, and the line finishes at 7.2 s and then holds for 2.4 s. Fix: pop the tiles every half beat (3000–5100), have the line complete by 6600 and dive at 7200. That saves one bar.

**7. On-screen copy that reads as machine-written.** At 11–14: "Mentoring with Priya at 16:00 is today, and three new notes." Two clauses are joined badly. Try "Good morning, Alex. Mentoring with Priya at 4, and three new notes." At 15–18 the flight row's "Live times unavailable" sits in the hero shot and reads as an error. Item 2's fix removes it.

**8. The logo is up too briefly (61.2–67.2).** The ending works: "the good days" over "How was today?", with Great pressed on the chord. But the lockup only completes at 65.3 and the fade starts at 66.9, about 1.6 s. Fix: start the lockup at 64.2.

**9. The pull-back wall (49.8–52.8).** The pointer sits still for 3 s. Let it drift across the wall, or cut a beat.

## Team beat: recommendation

**Where:** early afternoon, straight after Saved and before the evening run (it starts where Music starts now, at 34.8). It is already set up: the Ask screen at 18.5–20.5 shows the suggestion chip "Remind me to get something for Priya's birthday". This beat pays that off, and the rotation then calls back to "gift idea".

**How long:** 2 bars (4.8 s). Fund it with item 6 (−2.4), item 4 (−3.6) and item 3 (−2.4). Give 1.2 s of what's left back to the Ask hold and the rest to the logo.

**The single moment:** a "From Muse" card arriving on Home, framed tight on her cat avatar and one line: "Found Priya's [gift] at £34, £8 under the price when you saved it." The card pops in on a downbeat, holds 3 beats, then the pointer taps "Open it". The face reads instantly at phone size.

Avoid:
- **The Team roster** (07-team). It's three characters and three job lines to read, and "Turns Linear issues into to-dos" is work jargon.
- **The setup flow.** It's a process, not a payoff.
- **Branded sample gifts.** Don't use "Lego Ferrari" (from the polish shots). The brief bans other companies' marks. Use a generic gift, such as a record player or a moka pot.
