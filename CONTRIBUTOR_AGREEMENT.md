# Contributor Assignment Agreement

Version 1.0

Athena is free software under the GNU AGPL v3.0 or later. To keep the
project able to change its licence terms, offer a hosted service, or license
Athena to someone on other terms, its maintainer needs to own the copyright
in every part of it. This agreement transfers the copyright in your
contributions to the maintainer, and gives you a licence back so you can
still do anything you like with your own work.

Please read it before you agree. It's short, but it's a real legal
agreement. If you're not sure whether you can agree, ask before you
contribute: [athena@hojo.dev](mailto:athena@hojo.dev).

## Definitions

- **"You"** means the person, or the organisation they act for, who submits
  a Contribution and agrees to this agreement.
- **"We"** or **"the Maintainer"** means whoever owns the Athena project:
  the owner of the repository at `github.com/matthojo/athena` when you
  agree, and anyone the project is later transferred to.
- **"Contribution"** means any work of authorship (code, documentation,
  designs, images, translations, tests or anything else) that you submit to
  the project for inclusion, whether by pull request, patch, issue,
  comment or any other channel. Something you clearly mark as "not a
  contribution" isn't one.
- **"The Project"** means Athena and any other software or documentation
  the Maintainer publishes from that repository.

## 1. You assign your copyright to us

You assign to the Maintainer all right, title and interest worldwide in each
Contribution, including its copyright and every other intellectual property
right in it apart from patents (section 4), from the moment you submit it.

Where the law doesn't let you assign a right, or the assignment doesn't take
effect for any reason, you instead grant the Maintainer an exclusive,
perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use, copy,
change, publish, distribute, sublicense and otherwise exploit the
Contribution in any way, with the right to transfer that licence. This
licence is subject only to the licence we grant back to you in section 3.

## 2. Moral rights

To the extent the law allows, you waive, and agree not to assert, any moral
rights you have in your Contributions (such as the right to be named as
author, or to object to changes) against the Maintainer or anyone who
receives the Project from us. The Project's git history keeps your
authorship on record regardless.

## 3. A licence back to you

The Maintainer grants you a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, copy, change, publish, distribute
and sublicense your own Contributions, for any purpose and under any
licence you choose. Assigning your copyright doesn't stop you using your own
work.

## 4. Patents

You grant the Maintainer, and everyone who receives software the Maintainer
distributes, a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free,
irrevocable patent licence to make, have made, use, sell, offer for sale,
import and otherwise transfer your Contributions, alone or combined with the
Project, covering any patent claims you can license that your Contribution
would otherwise infringe. If anyone starts patent litigation claiming that
the Project, or a Contribution in it, infringes a patent, the patent
licences this section grants to them end on the date that litigation is
filed.

## 5. What we may do with your Contributions

The Maintainer may use, change, relicense and distribute Contributions on
any terms, including:

- under the GNU AGPL, another open-source licence, or a commercial licence;
- as part of a hosted or paid service, with or without publishing changes
  that only that service uses.

We don't have to use any Contribution, and nothing here obliges us to
include, maintain or credit it.

## 6. What stays open

Every version of the Project that we have published under an open-source
licence stays available under that licence. This agreement doesn't take
back the AGPL from anyone, for any version already released under it.

## 7. What you confirm

When you agree, and each time you submit a Contribution, you confirm that:

1. Each Contribution is your original work, or you otherwise have the right
   to assign it under this agreement.
2. If your employer, or anyone else, has rights in what you create (for
   example under an employment contract), they have allowed you to make the
   Contribution and assign it under this agreement, or they have agreed to
   this agreement themselves.
3. If part of a Contribution was written by someone else, or comes from
   another project, you say so in the pull request, naming its source and
   its licence. We only accept such material under a licence that lets us
   relicense it (MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC or similar). Never submit code
   under the GPL, AGPL or another copyleft licence as your own work.
4. If you used an AI tool or code generator, the tool's terms let you use
   and assign what it produced, you have reviewed that output, and you take
   responsibility for it as you would for anything else you submit.
5. As far as you know, nothing in the Contribution infringes anyone else's
   rights.

If any of this stops being true, tell us at
[athena@hojo.dev](mailto:athena@hojo.dev).

## 8. Confirming the assignment

If the Maintainer reasonably asks, you will sign any document needed to
confirm or record the assignment in section 1. The Maintainer pays any cost
of doing so.

## 9. No warranty

Apart from what you confirm in section 7, you give your Contributions "as
is", without warranties of any kind, and you are not responsible for
supporting them.

## 10. Scope

This agreement covers every Contribution you have submitted to the Project
and every one you submit after you agree, until you tell us in writing that
it no longer covers future Contributions. A later version of this agreement
applies to you only if you agree to that version.

## How to agree

On your pull request, post a comment containing this sentence on a line of
its own:

```
I have read the Contributor Assignment Agreement (version 1.0) and I agree to it.
```

The **Contributor agreement** check records your GitHub account, the time
and a link to the comment, and passes once everyone who wrote commits in
the pull request has agreed. You only need to agree once.

If you're contributing on behalf of your employer or another organisation,
someone who can sign for it must agree too: write to
[athena@hojo.dev](mailto:athena@hojo.dev) before you open the pull request.
